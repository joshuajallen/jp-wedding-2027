Recommended carriers and routes depending on where you're flying from.

From India

Flying from India

Direct flights:

  • Indigo & Kenya Airways: Mumbai-Nairobi (direct) - approx. $550

One-stop flights:

Qatar and Emirates offer flights to Nairobi with a layover in Doha or Dubai from all major cities (Kolkata, New Delhi, Mumbai)

From the UK

Flying from the UK

Direct flights (from London):

  • British Airways ($1400)
  • Kenya Airways ($900)

One-stop flights (from London):

Lufthansa, Turkish Airlines, KLM, Air France ($750-900)

Getting Around

Getting around Nairobi

Recommended

Uber

Widely available and the easiest way to get around. A ride from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) into the city averages around $15.
