Getting there

Travelling to the wedding venue

⚠️ IMPORTANT: DO NOT BOOK TRAINS OR TRANSPORT TO TAITA - We will organize and cover this for you!

We're happy to organize and cover the cost of train travel for your party to Taita Hills Safari Resort & Spa, and we'll share logistical details closer to the date. We'll also coordinate the transfer for all guests from Voi, the closest train station, to the resort — about a 45-minute drive.

If you'd prefer to investigate other options yourself, here's how to get to the lodge. Note: this covers the final leg to the venue for the wedding events on June 14–15. For information on getting to Kenya itself, see the Travel page.

Taita Hills Safari Resort & Spa is easily accessible by road, train and by air.

By Road

Driving to Taita Hills Safari Resort & Spa from Nairobi takes approximately 6 hours and 3 hours from Mombasa. It is a scenic drive through the Highlands of Kenya experiencing its varied biodiversity.

By Train

This unique journey by train travels through varied landscapes including Tsavo National Park. You can either take a morning train departing Nairobi at 8 a.m. and arrive at Voi train station at noon or take an afternoon train departing Nairobi at 3 p.m. and arriving at Voi train station at 6:48 p.m. Voi train station is closest to the resort, and from there, a 45 minute transfer will bring you to the resort.

By Air

The sanctuary private airstrip, which is served by both private charters and helicopters is within the resort proximity.
